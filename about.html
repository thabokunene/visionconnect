Our Story

Built on the corridor.

Vision Connect started with three trucks and one route: Johannesburg to Harare. Today we run eighteen corridors across eight SADC countries, moving dangerous goods, abnormal loads, bulk commodities, and containers.

We did not come from logistics as a spreadsheet exercise. We came from it as a working life. The founders drove the routes, stood at the border posts, and learned what fails and what works. That knowledge is still the operating system of this company.

We do not chase volume. We take the loads that require planning, permits, and precision. The cargo that cannot fail. That is where we are strongest, and that is where our clients need us most.

How We Work

Four rules.

01

Plan before we move

Every load gets a route plan, permit check, and risk assessment before a truck leaves the yard. No exceptions.

02

Document everything

Permits, tremcards, customs dockets, weighbridge certificates. If it is not on file, it did not happen.

03

Answer the phone

24 hours a day, seven days a week. A control tower that does not answer is not a control tower.

04

Own the outcome

When we accept a load, we accept responsibility for it. From collection to delivery, it is ours.

Temporary Driver Supply

Why hire our drivers.

Hiring drivers in Southern Africa is a liability problem dressed up as a recruitment problem. A permanent driver means payroll, benefits, CCMA exposure, upskilling cost, and fixed headcount through slow seasons. Our temporary driver service removes all of it. You get qualified drivers on your roster. We carry the risk.

01

Immediate cover when a driver is sick.

A driver calls in sick at 04:00. Your truck is loaded. The customer is waiting. That is not a scheduling problem, that is a revenue problem.

With our service, you call the control tower. We deploy a qualified driver to your depot. In most cases within the same working day. No scrambling to find cover. No cancelled loads. No angry client on the phone at 06:00.

Because we run a standing roster of vetted drivers across all major corridors, we always have spare capacity on the bench. You do not need to.

02

Replace a driver without inheriting a CCMA case.

This is the single biggest cost of permanent drivers that nobody puts on a balance sheet. When a permanent driver's conduct is a problem, you cannot simply replace them. You must follow process. Warnings. Hearings. Time. Legal fees. And if you get it wrong, the CCMA.

Under our temporary driver service, that burden sits with us. If you are not happy with a driver's conduct or performance, you notify us. We remove them and replace them. No disciplinary procedure on your side. No CCMA exposure on your side. No wrongful dismissal claim on your side.

We handle the employment relationship. You handle your business. That is the arrangement.

03

Scale up for peak season. Scale down when it slows.

Harvest season. Mining surge. Project cargo arriving at the port. Contract wins. These are good problems. They become bad problems when you hire permanent drivers you cannot afford to keep when the work dries up.

With temporary drivers, you add headcount when the work arrives and reduce it when the season ends. No retrenchment process. No severance liability. No bloated payroll through the quiet months.

You match your driver count to your revenue. Not the other way around.

04

Outsource driver upskilling and certification.

PrDP renewals. ADR certification. Medical fitness. Defensive driving. Cross-border documentation training. Every year these costs land on your desk, and every year they climb.

When you hire through us, upskilling sits with us. We keep our drivers current on the certifications your work requires. We track expiry dates. We arrange renewals. We run the training. You get a driver who walks in ready to work, with valid documentation on day one.

That is not a recruitment fee. That is a managed cost we absorb so you do not have to.

05

No payroll, benefits, or HR admin.

If you take drivers on our payroll, you issue one invoice a month. That is it. No PAYE. No UIF. No SDL. No provident fund. No leave liability. No overtime disputes. No HR files.

You pay for hours worked. Nothing more.

06

Trial a driver before you commit.

Hiring a permanent driver on a CV is a gamble. Hiring on a 30-day temporary placement is not. You see how they handle your routes, your equipment, your clients, and your standards before you offer them anything permanent.

If they work out, you can take them onto your payroll. If they do not, you notify us and we replace them. Either way, you have tested the fit before you have made a long-term commitment.

Capabilities

Five service lines.

Each service is built around the specific permits, compliance, and operational demands of Southern African freight. No shortcuts. No exceptions.

01 / Dangerous Goods

ADR Class 1 to 9.

We move hazardous cargo that most carriers won't touch. Explosives, gases, flammable liquids, oxidizers, toxic substances, radioactive material, corrosives, and miscellaneous dangerous goods.

Every load runs with ADR-trained drivers, correct placarding, tremcards, and full SABS and CBRTA documentation. Emergency response protocols are in place before the truck leaves the yard.

Payload Up to 34t per vehicle
ADR Classes Class 1 to 9
Permits SABS, CBRTA, ADR certification
Lead Time 48 hours minimum
Corridors 14 active routes

02 / Abnormal Loads

Overdimensional and over-mass.

Transformers. Generators. Industrial plant. Mining equipment. When cargo is too wide, too tall, too long, or too heavy for a standard rig, we move it.

Hydraulic lowbeds, extendable trailers, and route surveys for every bridge, overhead cable, and toll plaza. SANRAL permits, provincial exemptions, and escort vehicle coordination handled end to end.

Payload Up to 120t per convoy
Permits SANRAL, provincial exemptions
Lead Time 5 to 15 days
Escorts Pilot vehicles available
Corridors 10 active routes
Request an Abnormal Load Quote arrow_forward
03 / Cross-Border

Eight countries. One standard.

South Africa, Botswana, Namibia, Zambia, Zimbabwe, Mozambique, Malawi, and the DRC. We run the major SADC trade corridors and every critical border post between them.

CBRTA permits, TBP registration, SADC harmonised documentation, and customs pre-clearance at every crossing. Your freight moves without delays that kill schedules.

Payload Up to 34t per vehicle
Permits CBRTA, TBP, SADC harmonised docs
Lead Time 24 to 72 hours
Countries 8 SADC nations
Corridors 18 active routes
Request a Cross-Border Quote arrow_forward
04 / Bulk, Grain, and Minerals

Tippers and bulkers.

Maize, wheat, soya, fertiliser, copper concentrate, chrome, manganese, coal. We move agricultural and mineral bulk across the region.

Side-tippers, end-tippers, and bulk tankers. Weighbridge compliance at every load point. Full documentation for cross-border bulk commodity movement.

Payload Up to 36t volumetric capacity
Permits Bulk permits, weighbridge compliance
Lead Time 24 hours domestic
Trailers Side-tipper, end-tipper, bulk tanker
Corridors 12 active routes

05 / Containerised

FCL, LCL, SOC, breakbulk.

Standard 20ft and 40ft containers. High-cube for volumetric cargo. Shipper-owned containers. Breakbulk for cargo that doesn't fit in a box.

Port dockets and customs pre-clearance at Durban, Cape Town, Walvis Bay, Beira, and Maputo. Same-day loading when the container hits the dock.

Container Sizes 20ft, 40ft standard and high-cube
Permits Port dockets, customs pre-clearance
Lead Time Same-day on dock
Service Types FCL, LCL, SOC, breakbulk
Ports Durban, Cape Town, Walvis Bay, Beira, Maputo
